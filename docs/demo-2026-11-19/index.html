What this demo shows

Update 3, tasks 3 and 4. Open Appearance at the top right, and the brush button in the child's play screen.

  • Two styles, Soft and Sharp: Soft has no dark outlines, rounder corners, soft shadows and blurred background clouds. Sharp is the 15 Nov look: clear outlines, key-style buttons with a lower edge, no blur. Both work with every colour scheme. Soft is the default.
  • Parent and child choose separately: parents use Appearance (in the app: Account → Appearance); the child uses the brush button, My colours, on the play home. Older children often prefer Sharp. Nothing about colours or style is on the landing page.
  • Blur only where it is cheap: the drifting background clouds, the sun's glow and the soft hill edges. Cards stay sharp so text is easy to read and cheap phones stay smooth.
  • Every colour changes with the scheme: sky, sun, hills, trees, page clouds, Cloudy's outline and cheeks, buttons, chips, bars and the play screen. Nothing green stays on a purple page.
  • Six schemes: Warm Hills, Sky, Mint, Lilac, Peach and Night. The first five have their own dark version; Night is dark only.
  • Some colours never change: pictures (an apple stays red, a banana yellow) and colours that are part of a task (sort by colour). Changing them would teach the wrong thing or break the game.
  • Game targets are never blurred and keep a clear edge in every scheme, so children with weak sight can find them.

Contrast in this scheme

Text needs 4.5 or more; a game target's edge needs 3 or more.
